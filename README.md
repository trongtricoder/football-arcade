# Football Arcade

Football Arcade is a browser-based collection of fast football “what-if” games.

## Era XI

Era XI is the live game.

Choose a football era and formation, then build an eleven from rotating league-and-era player draws. Position fit, historical adaptability, individual playstyles, famous partnerships, club cores, and manager tactics all affect the team.

After the squad is complete, a manager and historical league season are drawn. The game simulates the complete league fixture list and produces:

- live matchday results and standings;
- a complete final table;
- player goals, assists, ratings, and awards;
- tactical and chemistry analysis;
- a final explanation of why the team succeeded or failed.

The simulation is deterministic: identical selections and game versions produce identical results.

## In development

Five-a-Side and Build-a-Player remain visible as previews but are locked while their game loops and simulation models are rebuilt.

## Football data

Football Arcade currently includes:

- 267 curated player versions across the 1980s–2020s;
- 49 managers;
- 230 historical top-five-league seasons;
- original player ratings and playstyle tags;
- versioned simulation and balance configuration.

Ratings are created for Football Arcade and are not copied from EA Sports FC, Football Manager, or another commercial game.
